Settings

The Operator Settings that apply to everything, with their defaults and ranges. At its top, the one place for setup: one entry per service and File Format of this build, then Account Mappings (S-40). Every number Slussen chooses itself is one of the Operator Settings, a setting on a service's page, or a value a part declares.

Setup starts at the top of Settings

Changed (S-40): Settings is the one place for setup. At its top, one entry per service and File Format of this build, in the order of setting up, then Account Mappings; each shows its status and opens its page; an entry that cannot be used yet says why (UC-013 BR-008, BR-015). There is no Connections level, no setup wizard and no separate Bank Accounts or Add Bank Account entry.
