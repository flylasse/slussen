UC-010 A5

Detailed Logging: what it includes and what it never includes.

Settings

Operator Settings

Upload Window

Switch on Detailed Logging?

Device logs will then contain transaction data and Bank Account numbers, for troubleshooting.

They stay on this device and are never in a device backup. Credentials, Bank Consent data and Bank Sessions are never logged, even then.

Cancel Switch on

Changing the Fetch Schedule states what the new value means.

Operator Settings

Upload Window

Fetch Schedule

2 · default 1 0 · off

Slussen will make one Scheduled Fetch in any 24 hours (the Fetch Schedule, 2 by default, an Operator Setting). A Scheduled Fetch only fetches and compares; it never uploads.

UC-010 A9

Switching Upload as Cleared.

Settings

Operator Settings

Upload Window

Switch Upload as Cleared off?

The change applies only to Upload Plans prepared from now on and changes nothing already in the Budget App.

While it is off, Slussen marks nothing Cleared, including Transfer Counterparts: clearing is left to you in the Budget App.

Cancel Switch off
