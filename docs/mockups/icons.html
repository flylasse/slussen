Slussen · mockup gate · icon review

App icon proposals

Concept A, Sluice, is the Operator's choice (2026-10-15). It is shown at launcher and notification sizes, under the three common masks, on light and dark home screens, and as a themed icon; B and C follow as not chosen.

Each proposal is an Android adaptive icon: a full-bleed background layer, a foreground layer whose artwork stays well inside the 66-unit safe zone of the 108-unit canvas (the geometry Android sets for adaptive icons), and a monochrome layer for themed icons. The launcher applies the mask, so no corners are drawn into the files. Placeholder icons on the home screens are neutral shapes, not real apps.

Chosen: A · Sluice

The Operator chose concept A on 2026-10-15, asking for a clearer arrow: its head now sits fully in clear space above the water and the gate, white with a dark halo, and reads the same in the themed icon.

A · Sluice

Two water levels and the lock gate between them; a white arrow with a dark halo passes over once, its triangular head in clear space above the water and the gate, so that it reads as an arrow at every size and in the themed icon. Slussen is the name of such a lock. File: icon-sluice.svg.
