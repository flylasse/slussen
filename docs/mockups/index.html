Operator decisions at the mockup gate

Every item that was open for the mockup gate is decided; nothing is left open.

  • The bottom navigation never disappears. The Upload Plan is a view inside its Budget's tab, opened by Review Upload Plan; every tap prepares a new plan from the current data (UC-006 BR-019, S-20).
  • Leaving the plan, by Back or by any tab including its own Budget's, discards it like Cancel: nothing is written. There is no background plan, no banner and no paused state on other screens (resolves M-05).
  • While shown, the plan states its expiry and nothing changes it. A transaction or Transfer can be tapped inside the plan; its Review Decision or Transfer Decision sheet opens on top, and after a choice the plan is rebuilt in place with a new expiry (UC-006 A3, UC-004 A8).
  • The other side of a Transfer is always a Budget Account Slussen fetches; a Funded By or Transfer Partner whose Budget Account lost its last Account Mapping no longer applies and is shown so (UC-005 BR-014, UC-009 step 1; resolves M-08).
  • UC-001 A13 stays as a safeguard but has no frame: the Fetch Schedule's range cannot reach the limit (M-10).
  • No UNDO snackbars and no Withdraw button (M-13). The invented household's resemblance to the Operator's structure is accepted (M-17).
  • The icons follow the adaptive icon geometry (M-18). The Operator chose concept A, Sluice, and asked for a clearer arrow, which is done (2026-10-15).
  • UC-007 step 6: a paste-back field for the result of Strong Customer Authentication, as in the predecessor, is acceptable though awkward; a helpful redirect back into Slussen is welcome but not mandatory. Decided in the slice that builds Bank Consent (O-P2-21).
  • UC-004 A10 and UC-006 A10 are kept: drawn and labelled for a replaceable Budget App, although they cannot occur with YNAB (O-P2-22).
  • Reading YNAB moves to the Budget header: "YNAB · read 13:21 ⟳", amber when a read fails; the Sources card shows only Sources. Pull to refresh fetches and then reads YNAB on the Overview, a Budget tab and a Bank Account. The Fetch Cooldown is set per Data Provider (S-22).
  • Many Budgets (mockups only, no spec change): up to three Budgets keep their own tabs; with four or more, the bar becomes Overview · Budgets · Settings, Budgets opens the last Budget viewed with the combined badge, and the Budget header is a switcher, following Material 3's three to five destinations.
  • S-23 and S-24: Budgets, Bank Accounts, Account Mappings, Start Dates and service credentials are set in the app (Setup, Account Mappings, Settings); an own release build needs only its signing values, which since S-25 are kept outside the repository in the standard Gradle places. Kestrel Old Savings is now a Bank Account without an Account Mapping.
  • Leaving the screen after confirming does not cancel: the Upload finishes and its outcome is shown when the Operator comes back (UC-006 BR-019 as changed by S-21).
  • Identifying a Bank Account again asks first only when a send through its Account Mapping was not rejected, as moving and removing do; rejected sends are sent again (UC-009 A6, A7, BR-007; O-P2-35).
  • While a confirmed Upload is being written, all setup is paused until it is written: credentials, Budgets and Bank Accounts, Account Mappings, Bank Consents and Operator Settings (UC-006 BR-005; O-P2-36).
  • A Data Provider whose credentials are missing while it has a Bank Account stays in use in Connections, with the status Credentials missing and its page reachable; Not set up lists only Data Providers with neither credentials nor a Bank Account (UC-014 BR-007; O-P2-37).
