Review Transfers

Adopted from the predecessor: a Transfer Review screen that never writes, one card per Transfer with its choices, and the Guessed Transfer banner that leads to it. Changed: Hold back is a choice for every Transfer that is not uploaded (UC-005 A2), a Guessed Transfer is not in any Upload Plan until confirmed instead of being written as the guess (UC-005 BR-010), and the other side is chosen as a Budget Account that Slussen fetches (UC-005 A3, BR-014).

This version: Transfers detected, not yet decided

The version that fetches and compares detects Transfers and gives their Transfer Legs their status, but has no Transfer Decisions and uploads nothing (transactions plan §1, O-T-01, O-T-02). Its Transfers screen is read only and stands next to the full-product states above (UX-D1). Each Transfer shows both sides with Bank Account, Budget Account, signed amount, booked date and whether it arrived; the counts say what they count.

UC-005 1 · A4, A5, A8 · BR-010

The Transfers of a Budget in this version, with both sides of each. A Guessed Transfer says why it is uncertain; a Lonely Transfer says what it waits for and until when; one whose other side did not arrive in time is shown as an ordinary Bank Transaction. Build state, not part of the use case: while Transfer Decisions (UC-005 steps 2–6) are not built, no card offers a choice; one line says that confirming comes in a later version, nothing is uploaded and no decision is needed now. The full product: Review Transfers.
