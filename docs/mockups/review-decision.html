Review Decision sheet

Review Decisions

Adopted from the predecessor: the decision chips Already Present, Import Anyway and Defer next to the doubtful row. Changed: the decision opens in a sheet that states why the transaction is doubtful, offers exactly the choices of UC-004 BR-001 for its Transaction Status, and says what each choice does to the next Upload Plan.
