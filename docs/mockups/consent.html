Grant, renew or revoke a Bank Consent

One Strong Customer Authentication per Bank, valid until the expiry the Bank or Data Provider reports.

Bank Consents

Adopted from the predecessor: one sheet for granting and renewing, opened from every place that shows a missing or expiring Bank Consent, with the steps in order and the result in the sheet. Changed: the steps follow UC-007 without assuming how the result returns (no paste field), the Consent Request's one-time use and lifetime are stated, revoking is confirmed and deletes everything stored, and the covered Bank Accounts are identified or sent to confirmation.
