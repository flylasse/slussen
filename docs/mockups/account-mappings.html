Bank Accounts and Account Mappings

Opened from its entry at the top of Settings, the one place for Bank Accounts (no separate Bank Accounts or Add Bank Account entry, S-40): first the Bank Accounts to map, then the mapped Bank Accounts by Budget, then two short read-only lists, Not imported and Kept by hand. Every Bank Account identified with certainty and sending its transactions to exactly one Budget Account; mappings changed only deliberately.

Bank Accounts to map

The imported Bank Accounts that are not yet added, one line each (UC-009 BR-014). Each opens the card of S-38 for one Bank Account, with only the free Budget Accounts of its currency and the full picker one tap away (UC-013 steps 9–15). The S-38 choice of what to bring in and its statement of numbers are gone: importing is a switch on each Data Provider's page.

UC-013 8 · BR-015 · UC-009 BR-014

Account Mappings on the first run, open because YNAB serves three Budgets and Bank Accounts are imported (UC-013 BR-015). First the Bank Accounts to map, one line each: name, last four, Source, currency, balance. Each opens the card for that one Bank Account; Map all goes through them in order. No form for a Bank Account of a Transaction File here: that is on the HSA CSV export page.
