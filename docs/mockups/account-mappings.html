Connector legend

Locked

A transaction was sent through the Account Mapping; its Budget Account never changes.

Mapped, not locked

Nothing sent yet; the Bank Account can still be identified again.

Needs attention

Waiting for confirmation, unrecognised, removed from the Build Configuration, configuration error or currency mismatch.

Retired

Not fetched, compared, matched or uploaded; can be restored.
