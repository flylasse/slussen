# Phase A-3: the Transfers of a Budget, read-only

Read `_common.md` in this folder first. Survey: `docs/reviews/build_out_sweep_A_screens_settings.md` (§2 UC-008, §3 row A-3) and `docs/reviews/build_out_sweep_B_decisions.md` §1 (what of UC-005 is built).

Read also: UC-005 (all of it; step 1, A4, A5, A8 and BR-010 are what this phase shows); UC-003 BR-014; UC-008; `docs/transactions_plan.md` §6 (S-T-20, S-T-21, O-T-10, O-T-11, UX-27, Phase 7b's notes); the mockup `transfers.html` (every state; build the permanent layout of `#list`, `#selected`, `#lonely`, `#first-card-payment`, `#doubtful-leg` and `#no-transfers` with their decision controls left out, since Transfer Decisions come in a later phase); the code of Phase A-2 and `slussen.core.transfer`.

Build:
1. A read model in `:core` for the Transfers of one Budget: each with its two Budget Accounts, amount, the dates of its Transfer Legs, whether both have arrived, whether it is a Guessed Transfer and why (more than one candidate, with the other candidates; or a card without a Funded By), whether it is eligible and if not why (a Transfer Leg that is Too Old or Before Start Date, a doubtful Transfer Leg), and for a Lonely Transfer the Budget Account of the expected other side and the date by which it must arrive. A Guessed Transfer that proposes no Budget Account has no stored Transfer (O-T-10): it is listed from its Bank Transaction. The number of Guessed Transfers of the Budget. Derived with the detection's own rules, never a second implementation.
2. The screen, reached from the Budget tab and from a Transfer Leg on the Bank Account screen: the list, a selected Transfer, a Lonely Transfer, no Transfers. The counts on the Budget tab that lead here agree with the list (UX-27).
3. Tests: core tests for the read model, with one Transfer of each kind the detection gives; ViewModel tests for every state; a screen IT for the list and a selected Transfer.

Not in this phase: every Transfer Decision (UC-005 steps 2-6).
