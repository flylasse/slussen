First, from the review of phase E1 (`docs/reviews/build_out_reviews/E1.md`, "For phase E2"):
- The Bank's row (`slussen.db`) and the Bank Consent (`device.db`) cannot share a transaction. Within one operation on the lane: consume the answer, complete at the Data Provider, find or create the Bank's row, then store the consent, once, only after the Data Provider accepted. When the completion is refused, when anything throws, and at a start after a process that ended in between, a Bank's row that this attempt created and that nothing refers to (no Bank Account, no Bank Consent, no Offered Bank Account, no Back-off) is removed, so that no Bank is left behind by a failed attempt (W6-0 item 8). A test for each of the three.
- The Bank's reference is refused in the core when it is longer than the entity model allows.
