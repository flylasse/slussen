# Phase B6: the screens of the decisions and the mappings

Read `_common.md` in this folder first. Surveys: `docs/reviews/build_out_sweep_B_decisions.md` (§2 B6 with the mockup state ids, §4 guess 10) and `docs/reviews/build_out_sweep_A_screens_settings.md` §4 question 1 (the `-this-build` states). The reviews of Wave 1 (`docs/reviews/build_out_reviews/A-1.md` to `A-5.md`) list what a reviewer found on the screens of that wave: do not repeat it.

Read also: UC-004, UC-005, UC-009 in full; NFR-023; the mockups `review-decision.html`, `transfers.html`, `transfer-budget-account.html`, `account-mappings.html` (every state, with the states S-B-04 and S-B-15 added and the corrections of S-B-10); the code of Wave 1's screens (`ui/bankaccount`, `ui/transfers`, the Budget tab) and of phases B2 to B5 (the core operations and their outcomes).

Build, in `:app`:
1. The Review Decision sheet on a Bank Transaction (from the Bank Account screen): the choices its Transaction Status offers, Already Present with the choice of the Budget App Transaction, change and clear, the locked state, each with its pending state and its answer at the control.
2. The Transfer Decisions on the Transfers screen: confirm, split, hold, choose another Budget Account with "Keep this answer?" (preselected as S-B-13 says) and the refusal of A10, a Lonely Transfer, the first Card Payment, a doubtful Transfer Leg leading to its Review Decision, the locked state. The read-only lines of Wave 1 ("decisions come in a later version", G-14) are replaced.
3. Manage Account Mappings: every state of `account-mappings.html` that phases B4 and B5 built, the read-only "set in a later version" lines of Wave 1 (G-10) replaced by the real Funded By and Transfer Partner.
4. The counts and "waiting for a decision" lines of the Budget tab and the Overview lead to where the decision is taken.
5. Tests first: JVM ViewModel tests for every state, with failing stores for NFR-023; screen ITs for one decision of each kind and one mapping change.

A decision that is refused, or that cannot be stored, is answered at its control and changes nothing. Nothing here writes to the Budget App.
