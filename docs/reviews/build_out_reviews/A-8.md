## To fix in this phase, with the orchestrator's decisions

1. **CONFIRMED. The tests of UC-010 BR-004 and UC-011 BR-002 cannot fail on the protection they name.** `aReviewDecisionIsStoredWhileTheJournalDatabaseFails` and `aTransferDecisionIsStoredWhileTheJournalCannotTrim` (`UC010DecisionJournalControlTest.kt:459, 475`) use `InMemoryDecisionJournal`, which throws from `record` at once; the real recorder never throws (it launches on its own thread, where its failures happen later). With the new catch in `DecisionJournal.hand` removed both still pass, since older catches take the throw; and if `RoomDecisionJournal.record` were made to block or to run on the caller's thread, no JVM test would notice. The one device test of the property, `aFailingJournalDatabaseDoesNotStopAStore`, asserts nothing about the journal, and closing the database probably does not make it fail (Room opens it again). The tests are made to fail when the protection goes: the recorder itself tested on the JVM with a write that fails and one that never returns, and the device test with a database that really fails.
2. **PLAUSIBLE. Settings waits for the journal's queue.** `JournalControl.shown()` reads the size on the journal's single thread (`RoomDecisionJournal.kt:451`), and `load()` and `showAgain()` (`SettingsViewModel.kt` ~227, ~247) wait for it before they publish anything. Case: a long trim, a clearing or a Fetch's many queued entries is under way: Settings does not open, and the answer to an unrelated change does not appear until the queue is empty (UC-010 BR-004: never delays an Operator action). Settings shows at once and the size follows when it is read.
3. **PLAUSIBLE. The switch is not fully ordered.** Callers on the coordinator's lane are ordered, but `ImportStatus.open()` (`ImportStatus.kt:117`) records the start of a session outside the lane and reads `journal_on` before it hands its entry over. Case: it reads on, then the switch queues "switched off" and stores off, then "Slussen opened" is queued: an entry after the last entry. The mirror case puts an entry before "switched on". One order for reading `journal_on` and handing an entry over, shared with the switch.
4. **PLAUSIBLE. Clearing and the trim after a lower limit run after the lane is released.** `JournalControl.kt:292-300, 252`. Clearing reads `journal_on` on the lane and clears later: if the Decision Journal is switched off in the gap, the clearing entry is written while it is off (A3: none while off); an Upload Plan prepared in the gap has its entry queued before the clearing, which removes it. Nothing an Upload needs is lost (the journal is only written, never read by it). The clearing and the trim are queued while the lane is held, and whether a clearing records is decided when it runs.
5. **CONFIRMED, minor. A false answer after a trim that failed part-way.** `JournalTrimming.keepWithin` commits each step in its own transaction; an exception in a later step leaves the earlier ones removed, and the screen says "oldest entries could not be removed now" (`JournalTexts.kt:173`). And the text when the journal is off says entries are removed "once the Decision Journal is on again", while the entry for switching on is written without a trim, so they go only at the next ordinary entry.

## For phase A-9 (in its prompt)

- A read of the newest entry straight from the DAO leaves out entries that are queued and not written yet: the read goes through the journal's own thread.
