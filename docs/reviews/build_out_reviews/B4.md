Independent reviewer, read-only, another model than the builder. **No defect that could lead to a duplicate, to something stuck for ever, or to a change reported as stored when it was not (or the reverse).**

1. **The engine (`Comparison.statusesAgainAfter`, `recomputePending`) and the move of `TransferDecisions` onto it: no defect.** `concludeFromStoredData()` calls the Comparison's own `conclude()`, `applyComparison` and `ComparisonRules.conclude`, not a copy (`Comparison.kt:254, 260`). The marker is set inside each change's own transaction (Room: `@Transaction` DAO methods, `MainDaos.kt:225-327`; the fake: `InMemoryStores.kt:402-453`) and cleared by O-5 in its own. A refused or failed change sets no marker; a marker an earlier stop left is concluded by the next status-again, by `recomputePending` at start and by a Comparison that reaches the Budget App. Phase B3's behaviour is unchanged (admission, all or nothing, journaling before the conclusions).
2. **Each change against its rule: no defect.** Retire and restore write only `retired`; a decided Transfer keeps its decision, stays out of every Upload Plan and comes back decided; an undecided one is released. A14 step 4: held and split Transfers survive, a Confirmed Card Payment whose type no longer fits is dissolved and journaled. The refusals of a Funded By or Transfer Partner match the entity model in Room and in the fake.
3. **`state_version`: no finding.** **Decision Journal: no finding.**
