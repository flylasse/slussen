# Review of phase E2 (006adac..f3ab584), Bank Consent in the core (UC-007)

Independent reviewer, read-only, a fresh context (Opus, as the builder: the plan's §5 row 3).

**No finding** that completes a consent from an answer that was not the Operator's own, leaks a one-time value, or removes a Bank that something refers to. Sound: the state (from the random source, 256 bits, only its digest stored; the lookup is an equality on the digest and nothing compares the state itself; the arrival time is taken before waiting for the lane; consuming is one transaction with its checks inside; a replayed, abandoned or replaced, late and foreign answer each have one outcome; a pasted address takes the same path); the two databases (the Bank's row is created only after the Data Provider completed, and removed on a refusal, an exception and a cancellation; a stored consent protects its Bank; the statement that removes an unused Bank covers every column of `slussen.db` that names a Bank, and `device.db`'s are checked first; at a start the dangling rows of `device.db` go before the left-behind Banks); nothing sensitive in logs, `toString`, results or the Decision Journal; the move of the fingerprint rule out of the Fetch is line for line the same; two Banks of one name in two countries stay two; admission in Normal only; the contract matches ADR-001 §4.1 and §4.5 word for word; the plug-in's unbuilt getter cannot be reached yet.

## To fix in this phase, with the orchestrator's decisions

1. **CONFIRMED on Room. A lasting reference already offered under another Bank of the same Data Provider stops all of this Bank's Offered Bank Accounts from being kept** (`BankConsents.kt:427`; the unique index of `offered_bank_account` on Data Provider and reference; the fake does not enforce it, so no test sees it). Case: one Bank's consent kept a reference; a second Bank's consent delivers it again; the insert breaks the index, the whole write is rolled back and turned into "no coverage", at every grant and renewal. A reference offered under another Bank is left out of what is kept and reported as not identified; the fake enforces the index.
2. **CONFIRMED. `Secret.sha256()` in `:plugin-api` is a way for any module to read a value derived from any secret**, a dictionary check for a secret of few possible values, and no rule guards it; the specification named no such function. The idea is sound for the state (256 random bits reveal nothing through a digest). **Decision:** it stays, behind its own opt-in that only `slussen.core.identity` may use, with an architecture rule that fails for any other caller; ADR-001 §4.1 and ADR-006 §2 name it as the one one-way derivation of a `Secret` (proposed as S-B-52).
3. **CONFIRMED. The state is written as hexadecimal, ADR-008 §7 says base64url without padding.** The code follows the ADR; the test asserts the rule, not the code.
4. **PLAUSIBLE. UC-007 A6's Blocked Reason is only a field of the result**: after a grant where an added Bank Account could not be identified with certainty, no screen shows it until a Fetch. **Decision:** the import status derives it from stored data, as UC-008 BR-001 asks: a Bank Account read through a Data Provider that needs a Bank Consent, whose reference is not among the Offered Bank Accounts kept for its Bank while that Bank has a Bank Consent, is shown as an unrecognised Bank Account. No new column.
5. **PLAUSIBLE. `renew` accepts any Bank** and would send the name of a Bank of another Data Provider or of a File Format to the plug-in. It is refused unless the Bank has a Bank Consent of this Data Provider or is the provider's Bank of one of its Bank Accounts (UC-007 BR-010).
6. **PLAUSIBLE, latent. With two Data Providers that need a Bank Consent, the completion checks the credentials of the first one whose plug-in reads a state**, not of the Data Provider the Consent Request belongs to. The request found by the digest names its Data Provider; that one's credentials and capability are used.
7. **PLAUSIBLE. A plug-in that throws** in the completion or the revocation leaves the call with an exception after the answer was consumed (the plug-in may not throw, ADR-001 §4.0: defence only). Guarded as the ending of the earlier session already is, with the outcome the specification gives a failure in transit.
8. **PLAUSIBLE. A failure while removing left-behind Banks at a start fails the whole start.** It is caught and the start goes on.
9. **Tests that cannot fail.** No test would fail if these were removed: the arrival time taken before the lane; the credential check before consuming; a reference under another Bank (item 1); a delivered account without a Bank's reference; the filter on an account reference's length; a failure while keeping the Offered Bank Accounts; each clause of the statement that removes an unused Bank (on Room: in the instrumented test); the removal under cancellation. The contract test has no case for an answer with two states, an empty state, or an error together with a code. Each gets its test.

## Decided in the specification

- An answer that arrives while the Data Provider's credentials are missing or refused is left unconsumed (it was never accepted; its lifetime still bounds it): UC-007 says so (S-B-52).

## For phases E3, E4 and E5 (in their prompts)

- E4: the completion is called as soon as the answer arrives; the intent's address is never logged; an earlier Bank Consent, an end not confirmed at the Bank and missing coverage are each shown as they are; renewing is offered only for a Bank with a consent.
