## To fix in this phase, with the orchestrator's decisions

1. **CONFIRMED, high (UC-003 BR-016; the "First" list).** A Review Decision changes every status of a Budget that owes a read, from stored data. `ReviewDecisions.change` (`ReviewDecisions.kt:180-190`) runs on the coordinator directly, not through `statusesAgainAfter`, so the guard in `Comparison.conclude` is never on its path; `ReviewRules.written` (`ReviewRules.kt:240`) concludes the whole Budget, and `RoomReviewStore.decide` (`RoomComparisonStores.kt:318`) stores all of it. Case (the set-up of `UC003PeriodReadOwedTest`): Upload Window raised 7 to 10, Household owes the read and TX-OLD (9 days ago) stays Too Old; the Operator decides on any other Household transaction: TX-OLD becomes New beside a copy that never covered its day. The decided transaction itself is wrong the same way. Transfer Decisions are narrower but also unguarded (`TransferDecisionRules.kt:356`). No test covers either path, and the BR-016 marker at `Comparison.kt:265` claims a guarantee this path breaks. **Decision (S-B-26, proposed):** while a Budget owes a read of its read period, no Review Decision and no Transfer Decision is taken on its Bank Transactions: the decision is refused with the reason, nothing is stored, and the read is offered.
2. **CONFIRMED, medium (showing something false).** `effectOf` (`GeneralSettings.kt` ~452) answers `StatusesGiven` even when a Budget owes the read and was skipped; `SettingTexts.kt:242` then says "Every Transaction Status given again from stored data". Case: Household gained a Budget Account (owes), Upload Window 7 to 10: Household's were not given again. The answer names the Budgets that wait for their read.
3. **PLAUSIBLE, medium (BR-016, money safety).** The flag is set only for served Budgets (`MainDaos.kt:47`; the fake too), and the `budget_read_state` row of a Budget no longer served is never removed. Case: Old Fund read with a cursor, then no longer served; the file upload window is raised (Old Fund not flagged); Old Fund is served and mapped again: its next read takes only the changes since its cursor, and the added days are never read. **Decision (S-B-21 amended):** the flag is set for every Budget that has a `change_cursor`, served or not.
4. **PLAUSIBLE, low, for Wave 4.** The guard keeps an owed Budget's Transfer Leg status but still applies released and dissolved Transfers of a concluded Budget whose Transfer Leg lies in the owed one; that Transfer Leg keeps a Transfer status without its Transfer until its Budget is read. Safe only if W4-09 blocks every Upload Plan of the owed Budget, not only per Transfer: recorded in W4-09.
5. **PLAUSIBLE, low (latent).** `RoomOperatorSettingsStore.storeFetchPeriod` (`RoomStores.kt:120`, also `InMemoryStores.kt:139`) stores any fetch period with no follow-up: a raise through it would skip the flag. Only a test calls it: remove it from production code or give it the follow-up.
6. **CONFIRMED, low.** Saving the same value again stores it, raises `state_version` and journals "changed from 7 to 7". **Decision:** an unchanged value stores nothing, records nothing and is answered as unchanged.
7. **Minor.** `SettingsViewModel` does not reload on `services.ended` as the Budget tab does. `UC003PeriodReadOwedTest.aMarkerLeftForAnotherReason…` names "the other Budget is concluded" but its set-up does not show that (the transaction was already New). Nothing tests a Review Decision or Transfer Decision on a Budget that owes a read.
