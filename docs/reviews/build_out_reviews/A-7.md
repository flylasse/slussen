## To fix in this phase, with the orchestrator's decisions

1. **CONFIRMED. Journal entries can be read wrongly** (`DescriptionLists.kt:258-270`, `record`, `joined`). Entries are joined with " · ", lists with "; ", an empty list is written "no entry", and nothing marks where an entry begins and ends. Case: the Card list [AUTOPAY], add "PAY · CARD": the journal reads "after: AUTOPAY · PAY · CARD", three entries to the eye. Each entry is written so that it cannot be taken for two or for the words around it.
2. **CONFIRMED. An entry of only `*` is accepted and can never match** (`DescriptionLists.kt:120-128`, `TransferDescriptions.kt:24-25`); the answer says "Saved … detected again". **Decision (S-B-27, proposed):** an entry holds at least one character other than `*`; one that does not is refused at the field with the reason.
