# Review of phase W2-0 (commit 5e1793e), the specification for Wave 2 (S-B-02..S-B-15)

Independent reviewer, read-only, another model than the writer. Findings ranked most serious first. The orchestrator's decision follows each where one was needed.

1. **CONFIRMED, a transaction stuck for ever.** UC-004 BR-004's new last sentence ("The Review Decision on a Transfer Leg is locked as well once the Transfer Decision of its Transfer is locked"), the new A7 trigger and the `REVIEW_DECISION` constraint lock a Transfer Leg whenever any Transfer Leg has a Sent Record other than Rejected (UC-005 BR-008). This contradicts BR-004's own middle sentence, A5 and A10, and UC-005 A9 step 2. Three cases have no way out: (a) a linked transfer that was uploaded and then deleted or merged in the Budget App: Re-upload is never offered, so the transaction is lost; (b) after Split, one Transfer Leg is uploaded as ordinary and the other is Needs Review or Possible Import By Other Path: it can never be decided and is never uploaded; (c) a linked Transfer Leg that is Outcome Unknown and waits for a decision can never be decided. **Correction:** keep only BR-004's first rule (locked while Already In Budget App through a send). A Transfer Leg is locked with its Transfer only while that Transfer Leg is itself Already In Budget App or Arrived As Transfer Counterpart. A Split Transfer Leg that has not been sent is never locked.
2. **CONFIRMED, duplicate.** UC-005 BR-005 joins to a decided Lonely Transfer any Bank Transaction "that has not been uploaded and is a Transfer Leg of no Transfer that has a Transfer Decision". So it can come from a Proposed Transfer whose other Transfer Leg was already uploaded as a linked transfer (it is then that Transfer's counterpart side), against BR-015's last sentence and BR-008: the decided Lonely Transfer would upload a second counterpart into its Budget Account. BR-005 also never says what happens to the Proposed Transfer it leaves, and the entity model's `TRANSFER` join constraint has no conditions. **Correction:** BR-016's wording in both places: "of no Transfer that has a Transfer Decision or an uploaded Transfer Leg; that Transfer is dissolved and its other Transfer Leg detected again". Sub-case, PLAUSIBLE: the arriving side was already uploaded as an ordinary transaction. **Orchestrator's decision (from Q-07 and Q-11):** a Bank Transaction already uploaded as an ordinary transaction is no Transfer Leg and never the other side; when the other side of a decided Lonely Transfer turns out to be such a transaction, the Transfer's ground is no longer valid (BR-015): it is dissolved and journaled, and its Transfer Leg becomes an ordinary Bank Transaction.
3. **CONFIRMED, a gap with a duplicate if a builder falls back to "Lonely".** UC-005 BR-016, the A10 trigger and ADR-002 O-13 sort the candidates into free, taken from a Proposed Transfer none of whose Transfer Legs is uploaded, or refused (uploaded, or in a decided Transfer). A candidate that is not uploaded itself but sits in a Proposed Transfer with an uploaded Transfer Leg fits none. **Correction:** add "or is a Transfer Leg of a Transfer one of whose Transfer Legs has been uploaded" to the refusal reason in BR-016, A10 and O-13.
4. **CONFIRMED gap.** UC-005 A3 step 4 and BR-016 "detect Transfers again" for released Transfer Legs and the other Transfer Leg of a dissolved Transfer; ADR-002 O-13 stores only the new side, the removal and the Funded By, and `comparison_pending` covers only UC-009 BR-015 and UC-010 step 5, so a stop or an Upload Plan can see released Transfer Legs with stale statuses. **Correction:** add UC-005 BR-016 (and the join of BR-005) to the `comparison_pending` sentence in O-13 and ADR-007 §3a.
5. **PLAUSIBLE, existed before, S-B-06 builds on it.** A Re-upload decision stays stored after its send is Written. If the transaction is deleted again in the Budget App, UC-003 BR-001's "unless the Operator chose Re-upload" makes it New again without a fresh choice. **Correction (the operator's answer Q-09, Re-upload once):** a Re-upload decision covers only the send it caused; a later deletion or merge gives Deleted In Budget App or Merged In Budget App again.
6. **PLAUSIBLE, order left open.** UC-005 BR-005 does not say which of two decided Lonely Transfers (same amount, same missing Budget Account) one arriving Bank Transaction joins. **Correction:** the nearest booked date, then the one decided first.
7. **CONFIRMED, minor.** UC-004 BR-001: "Deferred offers changing or clearing the decision" against the new "when that status offers no choice, it offers clearing only". **Correction:** qualify the Deferred sentence.
8. **PLAUSIBLE, entity model.** `TRANSFER.outflow_budget_account_id` "is filled as soon as Slussen proposes a Budget Account": for a Split or Held Transfer with an empty outflow side a later Funded By would fill it, against UC-005 BR-015. **Correction:** add "while `decision` is Proposed".
9. **Risk in an assumption of the writer.** Retiring a Bank Account dissolves a decided Transfer (the survey's reading of Q-11); after Restore, a Held Transfer comes back as a Proposed Transfer that is eligible without any decision. **Orchestrator's decision:** retiring does not dissolve a decided Transfer. While the Bank Account of one of its sides is a Retired Account the Transfer keeps its decision and is in no Upload Plan; restoring the Bank Account brings it back as decided. An undecided Transfer is released as before.

The other assumptions hold as conservative: Q-11, Q-12, Q-13 are written as answered (Q-12 extended to Held and Budget Account Chosen); a Transfer Decision locks on any Sent Record that is not Rejected; A10 also refuses when the only candidates are already uploaded (Split and Hold remain); UC-003 BR-005 allowing Re-upload of a merged transaction only aligns it with UC-004 A5 and BR-001. No findings: the entity model against the rules beyond 1, 2 and 8; the mockups; AGENTS.md §2.
