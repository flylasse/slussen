## To fix in this phase

- **1a (medium, CONFIRMED). A Fetch served from the Fetch Cooldown lists Bank Accounts it did not serve, and leaves out ones it did.** `FetchHistory.kt:146-151` takes every stored Bank Account of the Data Provider. The Fetch's own selection (`FetchTransactions.selected`, UC-001 step 3) only takes Bank Accounts that have an Account Mapping, are not Retired Accounts and have a provider reference, and it includes balance-only Bank Accounts (BR-011). Case: Kestrel Reserve was retired yesterday; today's cooldown Fetch still shows "Kestrel Reserve · Served · From the Fetch of …", and the list says "served from earlier Fetches: 3". Case: a file-imported Bank Account whose balance comes from this Data Provider is missing. Use the same filter as `selected()`.
- **1b (medium, CONFIRMED). A Fetch within the Fetch Cooldown with the Request Limit reached.** `FetchTransactions.kt:155-158` then records Served From Fetch Cooldown with no Fetch Results, while a Bank Account not read since it was added (UC-001 A2 step 1, BR-006) was not read at all. FetchHistory lists that Bank Account as `Served(null)`: "From an earlier Fetch" and "Nothing needed now". In fact nothing of it is stored, and A2 says to show "Request Limit reached". `UC008RecentFetchesTest.servedListsOnlyTheBankAccountsThereAtThatTime` (`:201-211`) asserts exactly this `Served(null)` state. List such a Bank Account as not read, held by the Request Limit, and correct the test.
- **2a (low, CONFIRMED). No action at the line for a whole-Fetch remedy (UC-008 A6).** The latest Fetch that Failed, was refused its credentials or was Not Consented says "Still needed now: Kestrel Feeds, Fetch failed (14:30): fetch again". It has no Bank Accounts, so `opens = false` (`RecentFetchesViewModel.kt:154`): the line cannot be tapped and has no button. Only per-Bank Account needs get the control.
- **4a (low, PLAUSIBLE). One old Fetch can make the whole list unreadable.** The `sourceBlocked` lambda (`ImportStatus.kt:200`) uses `dataProviders.single`, which throws for a stored Fetch of a Data Provider this build no longer has; the ViewModel catches it, but the whole list then shows "could not be read". Use `firstOrNull` and give no Blocked Reasons.
- **5.** No test covers a Retired Account or a balance-only Bank Account under a cooldown Fetch, a cooldown Fetch stopped by the Request Limit, or a Bank Account not recognised that is still mapped. The DisplayName of `accessLimitIsAWaitNotAnAsk` says "A19 … not delivered", but it tests Access Limit Reached.
- **6.** The cooldown KDoc (`FetchHistory.kt:144`) says "the Bank Accounts … that were there when the Fetch ran", which is the overreach of 1a.

No findings: storage (not touched); other wrong "still needed now" or "nothing needed now" lines ("still needed only for the latest word" traced against `lastResult` and `resultBlocked`, Access Limit Reached counting as a wait); the two screen ITs' set-up can produce what they assert.
