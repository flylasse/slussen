# Review of phase B6 (65da58c..bb07200), the screens of the decisions and the mappings

Independent reviewer, read-only, a fresh context (Opus, as the builder: the plan's §5 row 3).

Every result of the core is mapped to a text that says what is true (`ReviewOutcome`, `UnreadableOutcome`, `TransferOutcome`, `MappingChangeOutcome`, `MoveOutcome`, `IdentifyOutcome`); the numbers of a confirmation come from the core and are passed back unchanged, never filled in by the screen; locks hide every action. Nothing on screen claims the four states that wait for Sent Records. The three screen ITs' set-up, tags and texts match the screens.

## To fix in this phase

- **F1 CONFIRMED (most serious). An answer and a sheet land on the wrong Bank Transaction or Transfer.**
  - Review Decision: `BankAccountViewModel.kt` `decide()`/`unreadable()`. Case: line A, Import Anyway while a Comparison runs ("waits for: Reading the Budget App"); Close is not disabled; open line B: `open()` then `forgetReview()` clears `reviewPending`, so B's controls are live. When A's call ends, `reviewAnswer`, `chooser` and `readReview(A)` are written with no check that A is still open, and the unconditional `publish()` shows line B with A's sheet, A's choices and "Review Decision stored: Import Anyway · Transaction Status now New". B was not changed. Choosing Already Present on B then offers A's resembling Imports. NFR-023, and a decision shown as stored that was not.
  - Transfer Decision: `TransfersViewModel.store()` has the same flaw: `close()` clears `pending` and the result writes `answer` onto whatever Transfer is open; on a stored A3 choice `selected = sheet.staysWith` jumps the Operator back to the first Transfer.
  - Mappings: `AccountMappingsViewModel.closeSheet()` sets `pending = null` mid-run, so a second change can start on another Bank Account.
- **F2 CONFIRMED. Effect texts promise what UC-004 BR-009 and S-B-17 forbid** (`ReviewDecisionTexts.kt` `effect()`, `reUpload()`).
  - A Transfer Leg of a held Transfer that is Needs Review or Possible Import By Other Path still offers Import Anyway and says "rejoins its Transfer, which is then eligible as one linked transfer"; held, it is in no Upload Plan.
  - A Transfer Leg that already has a Re-upload decision while its Transfer is held shows the switch as "on: this Transfer Leg rejoins its Transfer".
  - A Deleted or Merged In Budget App Transfer Leg (not split) gets "switch it on to let this Transfer Leg rejoin its Transfer"; BR-009 says it is sent as a new transaction of its own Budget Account, not as a linked transfer.
  - The `UC-004 BR-009` marker above `effect()` names a rule the code below breaks.
- **F3 PLAUSIBLE (G-24).** `TransfersTexts.LONELY_JOINS` and `DECIDED_LONELY_JOINS` say a Bank Transaction "that arrives later joins it". Confirming or holding can pair it at once with an arrived side from a pair Slussen had proposed, which is then dissolved; the stored answer does not say that a pairing happened or that another Transfer was dissolved.
- **F4 CONFIRMED, minor.** `BankAccountTexts.decided()`: Already In Budget App as stored, when the decision is on the other Transfer Leg (`decisionInItsTransfer`), says "Already Present by your Review Decision" as if it were this line's own. Deferred handles the other side; this one does not.
- **F5 CONFIRMED.** `AccountMappingsViewModel` has no `services.ended` collector: the list and an open sheet are not read again when a Fetch or a decision ends elsewhere ("Waiting for your confirmation", "Sent through", offered Bank Accounts go stale).
- **F6 CONFIRMED, minor.** In the mapping editors Cancel and back stay enabled while pending; cancelling during a move whose answer is the confirmation of numbers leaves a question whose Confirm does nothing (`moveTo` and `identifyWith` return early once `editor` is null).
- **F7 CONFIRMED.** Back does not return to the open Transfer: `SlussenActivity.kt` `LaunchedEffect(current) { vm.load(current.selectLeg) }` runs again when the Transfers screen comes back to the top. Case: a Transfer, "Decide on the Transfer Leg" (A9) or "Change or clear the Funded By…", then back: the list shows with the Transfer closed.
- **F8 PLAUSIBLE, minor.** The Bank Account card "Decide on the N waiting…" counts Guessed Transfer legs; a credit card whose only waiting lines are a first Card Payment leads to a screen where no decision can be taken, only "Open its Transfer".
- **Tests.** `UC005TransferDecisionsViewModelTest` L149: `screen.ui().answer ?: screen.decision().answer` accepts the answer in either place, so it cannot catch an answer that left its control. No test covers F1 (close, or open another line, while pending), S-B-17 on the sheet (a Transfer Leg of a held Transfer: no Re-upload, its effect texts), Decision No Longer Holds, or the Locked, NotOffered and ChooseTransaction answers.
