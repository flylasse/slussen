## To fix in this phase

- **2a (medium).** `BankAccountTexts.kt:151-157` with `BankAccountTransactions.kt:480`: `writesIt` only says which side is the outflow and ignores UC-005 BR-010 and BR-013. Case: outflow dated 10-07 (Too Old on 10-15), inflow dated 10-09 with status Transfer Leg. The inflow says "the other side would carry it, so this one is not counted as Eligible", but the whole Transfer is in no Upload Plan; the Too Old outflow's sheet says "this side would carry it". The same when the other Transfer Leg is Needs Review. The line must give the real reason.
- **2b (low to medium).** `BankAccountTransactions.kt:503-508`: the fetched-empty text takes its dates from today's fetch period, not the one that Fetch asked for (S-T-11). Case: a Fetch at 30 days brings nothing, then the Operator saves 90 days: the screen says "No Bank Transactions from 2026-07-17 … for the fetch period (90 days)". The core test asserts exactly this (`UC008CheckImportStatusTest.kt:856`).
- **2c (low).** `TransferDetection.kt:146`: O-T-11 also names a guessed other side as fact. Case: a card without a Funded By, one paying Bank Account, an inflow past the grace period: "Its other side, from Checking, did not arrive", though BR-002 only proposes Checking.
- **2d (low).** `BankAccountTexts.kt:158`: a stored Lonely Transfer whose grace ended since the latest Comparison says "its other side is expected by 10-14" on 10-17; the "as the latest Comparison left it" fallback does not cover it.
- **2e (low).** `BankAccountTexts.kt:64`: "no longer applies, since no Bank Account is mapped to it" is also shown when the mapped Bank Account is a Retired Account or in another currency.
- **4a (low to medium).** `SlussenActivity.kt:106,161-164` with `BankAccountViewModel.kt:104-113`: system back with a sheet open leaves the Bank Account instead of closing the sheet; the ViewModel is kept by key and `load()` does not clear `openLine` or `statusesOpen`, so opening that Bank Account again shows the old sheet and not the list.
- **4c (note).** `#unmapped` and `#retired` have no way in yet (`ImportStatus.kt:97` lists only mapped Bank Accounts that are not Retired Accounts). An empty one would say "no Fetch of this Bank Account has succeeded. A successful Fetch brings them in" (`BankAccountTransactions.kt:505`), false for a Retired Account.
- **5a.** No test reaches `StatusReason.AsStored` on any branch (In The Future whose date came, a changed Start Date, a widened window).
- **5b.** No test for 2a, for the Lonely Transfer line text, for the general sheet (Why / What it means / Eligible), for the unsupported-Slussen-Import sheet, or for 4a.
- **5c.** `UC008CheckImportStatusScreenIT.kt:244`: `assertDoesNotExist("Import Anyway")` on the Match For Typed Entry sheet cannot fail; that sheet never names it.
- **6a.** `Texts.kt:63-68`: the KDoc of `UPLOADS_NOT_IN_THIS_BUILD` now sits over `OPEN_BANK_TRANSACTIONS`, and the constant it describes has none.
- **6b.** Tests on Funded By, Transfer lines and Retired Accounts carry `businessRules = ["BR-007"]` (One Budget at a Time), which they do not test (`UC008CheckImportStatusViewModelTest.kt:541,688`).
